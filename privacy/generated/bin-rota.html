Owner and Data Controller

John Theodore Reacher, trading as TeapotSoftware, United Kingdom.

Owner contact email: teapotsoft@outlook.com

This policy covers the Bin Rota app ("this Application"). In this policy "Users" means the people who use it.

In short

  • Everything you enter stays on your phone. Bin Rota has no account, no server and no cloud database, and the Owner never receives your data.
  • The app reads your contacts only when you choose someone from the contact picker, and copies only that person's name and phone number.
  • Reminder texts go from your own phone to the people on your rota, through your mobile network.
  • To get collection dates, the app sends your property reference (UPRN) or calendar link to your council's collection service.
  • There are no ads, analytics or trackers.

Device permissions for Personal Data access

This Application requests certain permissions from Users that allow it to access the User's device Data as described below.

  • Contacts (READ_CONTACTS): Used only when you tap to pick a person from your contacts. The app reads that one contact's name and phone number and saves them to the rota. It never uploads or scans your address book.
  • Send SMS (SEND_SMS, direct-download version only): Sends the reminder texts from your own SIM at the time you choose. Normal text charges from your network apply. The Google Play version does not ask for this: it shows a notification and opens your Messages app with the text ready for you to send.
  • Notifications: Shows reminder and status notifications on your phone. Notifications are created on the device; no push service is used.
  • Alarms, run at start-up and network access: Schedule the reminder for the right time, restore it after the phone restarts, and fetch collection dates.

Services the app contacts

This Application connects directly to these services. They are not processors of the Owner; their own privacy policies apply to what they receive. Third-party services contacted by this Application may collect files that record the interaction (System logs) or use other Personal Data (such as the IP Address) for operation and maintenance.

  • Greater Cambridge Shared Waste Service collection calendar: Provides bin collection dates when you enter a gc: council reference. What it receives: Your property's UPRN and your device's IP address. Location: United Kingdom (served through Microsoft Azure).
  • The calendar (iCal) address you enter, if any: Provides bin collection dates from a council calendar feed. What it receives: A request to that address, with your device's IP address. Location: Wherever that calendar is hosted.

Who we share data with

The Owner does not use any Data Processors for this Application.

Analytics and advertising

This Application contains no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking services, and uses no Trackers.

Backups

If Android backup is turned on for your Google account, Android may include the app's settings (including rota names and numbers) in your device backup, so they can be restored to a new phone. Google stores those backups under your account and its own privacy policy; the Owner cannot see them. You can turn this off in your phone's backup settings.

Deleting your data

Because your data is only on your device, you can delete it at any time: remove people in the app, clear the app's storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. The Owner holds no copy to delete.

To ask us to delete any data we hold about you, email teapotsoft@outlook.com.
